HARD RUNTIME CONTRACT
- Deliver exactly 10 separate transparent PNG files.
- Each file MUST be exactly 96x80 pixels.
- Filenames MUST be: enemy_0.png through enemy_9.png.
- Hard-edged authored pixel art. No painterly brushwork, no soft airbrush shading, no semi-realistic render, no anti-aliased silhouette edges, no CSS/procedural-looking geometry.
- Keep a coherent small runtime palette and clusters of deliberate pixels. It should sit beside the existing player sprite without looking downsampled from a painting.
- Preserve the chunky gothic-Halloween storybook identity and readable silhouettes.
- Orange = mundane lantern/fire light. Violet = necromancy/occult energy.
- Left/right is handled by Godot sprite flip; draw one facing direction consistently.
- Keep the character fully inside each 96x80 frame with several pixels of safety around the silhouette.
- Maintain consistent visual scale and anchor placement across all 8 frames. Grounded humanoids should share a stable foot baseline near the bottom of the frame. Flying enemies should share a stable body center instead.
- No labels, borders, grids, UI, backgrounds, drop shadows, or captions in final exported frames.

sit between them only in file-index order, not in playback order. Draw frames
8/9 as the natural continuation of the stride/wingbeat started in 4/5 (mirror
leg/wing), not as anything related to the attack pose.

The reference image establishes design, silhouette, props, palette, and personality only. Redraw it as crisp production pixel art; do not simply resize/downsample the reference.

ARCHETYPE-SPECIFIC DIRECTION
Hook Brute — huge slow front-line threat. Massive hooded/stitched corpse with an oversized butcher/cargo hook and rope/chain details. He should feel heavy rather than fast. Keep the head/face small relative to the body and make the hooked weapon dominate attack frames.

Frame specifics:
0 dead: huge body collapsed sideways with hook/rope sprawled.
1 hurt/rise-react: shoulders jolt back, hook dips, readable stagger without becoming small.
2 idle A: hunched heavy breathing stance, hook low.
3 idle B: weight shifts / shoulders settle; distinct silhouette.
4 movement A: lumbering lead-foot step.
5 movement B: second heavy stride with hook dragging/swinging.
8 movement C: lead-foot step continues, mirrored from frame 4, full weight commitment.
9 movement D: hook trails through its widest drag point, completing the lumbering cycle.
6 attack windup: hook pulled dramatically behind/above body.
7 attack release: broad committed hook swing with clear dangerous arc.
