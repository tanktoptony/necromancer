HARD RUNTIME CONTRACT
- Deliver exactly 10 separate transparent PNG files.
- Each file MUST be exactly 96x80 pixels.
- Filenames MUST be: enemy_0.png through enemy_9.png.

- Preserve the chunky gothic-Halloween storybook identity and readable silhouettes.

- Keep the character fully inside each 96x80 frame with several pixels of safety around the silhouette.
- Maintain consistent visual scale and anchor placement across all 8 frames. Grounded humanoids should share a stable foot baseline near the bottom of the frame. Flying enemies should share a stable body center instead.

ARCHETYPE-SPECIFIC DIRECTION
Hanged Sailor — lanky nautical undead associated with rigging/noose imagery. Keep recognizable sailor clothing, long dead weight, and unsettling rope/noose relationship. He should not look like an ordinary zombie. The runtime movement is ground/leap oriented, so frames 4-5 must still work as movement on deck even though the theme is hanging/rigging.

Frame specifics:
0 dead: collapsed sailor corpse with slack rope/noose detail.
1 hurt/rise-react: jerking recoil as if rope/body suddenly catches tension.
2 idle A: lanky hanging/slumped posture.
3 idle B: eerie sway or head/shoulder drift; distinct frame.
4 movement A: awkward sailor shuffle/drag with long limbs.
5 movement B: opposite stride/sway.
8 movement C: shuffle/drag continues, mirrored limb from frame 4.
9 movement D: completes the awkward gait cycle, long limbs settling before repeat.
6 attack windup: crouches/coils as if rope tension or body momentum is about to launch him.
7 attack release: committed leap/swinging strike pose, readable horizontal threat.
