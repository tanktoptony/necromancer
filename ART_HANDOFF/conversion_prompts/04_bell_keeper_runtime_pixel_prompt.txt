- Deliver exactly 10 separate transparent PNG files.
- Each file MUST be exactly 96x80 pixels.

ARCHETYPE-SPECIFIC DIRECTION
Bell Keeper — eerie support enemy. Robed, hooded, violet eyes, hand bell/staff motif. The bell itself may have mundane metal/warm highlights, but occult ring/haze from the ability should be violet. Silhouette should be taller and more ritualistic than Lantern Tosser.

Frame specifics:
0 dead: robes collapsed, bell on deck, violet eyes extinguished.
1 hurt/rise-react: body twists/recoils; bell pulled off-line.
2 idle A: stooped ritual stance, bell down.
3 idle B: bell slightly raised / head tilt with violet eyes visible.
4 movement A: slow shuffling step.
5 movement B: opposite shuffle, robes trailing differently.
8 movement C: shuffle continues, mirrored leg from frame 4, bell sways further.
9 movement D: robes settle back, completing the slow processional gait.
6 attack windup: bell raised, violet energy beginning to gather tightly around hand/bell.
7 attack release: bell ring/cast release with compact violet ring/burst integrated into sprite frame; do not fill the whole 96x80 canvas with fog.
