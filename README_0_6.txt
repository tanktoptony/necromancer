NECROMANCER: FIRST DAWN — VANIA PASS 0.6
COMBAT & COMMAND PASS

CONTROLS
A / D or arrows    Move
SPACE / W / Up     Jump
F / left click     Three-hit melee combo
E                  Raise corpse / claim item
Q                  Grave Hook to violet ring; pulls light enemies when no ring is targeted
C                  Cycle army command: FOLLOW / HOLD / ASSAULT
M                  Castlevania map
H                  Help
T                  Dismiss current message
R                  Restart at checkpoint
F3                 Collision overlay

0.6 FEATURES
- Three-hit player combo with a stronger third hit
- Hit-stop, screen shake, weapon arcs, impact bursts, and synthesized sound effects
- Enemy targeting can switch to threatening raised allies
- Melee attack-slot coordination reduces dogpiling
- New Hook Brute and Bell Wretch enemy roles
- Bell Wretches buff nearby enemies; kill or interrupt them first
- Grave Hook can pull light enemies into melee range
- Army commands: Follow, Hold, and Assault
- Raised corpses retain a role:
  Guard = balanced melee
  Brute = durable heavy melee
  Sentry = ranged soul projectile
- Followers have stronger braking and arrival behavior to reduce sliding
- Friendly units can be damaged and destroyed by enemies

ROLE COLORS
White/blue guard: balanced melee follower
Rust-colored large guard: brute follower
Pale-blue smaller guard: sentry follower
Gold enemy: Bell Wretch support unit
Large rust enemy: Hook Brute
