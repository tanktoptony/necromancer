HARD RUNTIME CONTRACT
- Deliver exactly 10 separate transparent PNG files.
- Each file MUST be exactly 96x80 pixels.
- Filenames MUST be: enemy_0.png through enemy_9.png.
- Hard-edged authored pixel art. No painterly brushwork, no soft airbrush shading, no semi-realistic render, no anti-aliased silhouette edges, no CSS/procedural-looking geometry.
- Keep a coherent small runtime palette and clusters of deliberate pixels. It should sit beside the existing player sprite without looking downsampled from a painting.
- Preserve the chunky gothic-Halloween storybook identity and readable silhouettes.
- Orange = mundane lantern/fire light. Violet = necromancy/occult energy.
- Left/right is handled by Godot sprite flip; draw one facing direction consistently.
- Keep the character fully inside each 96x80 frame with several pixels of safety around the silhouette.
- Maintain consistent visual scale and anchor placement across all 8 frames. Grounded humanoids should share a stable foot baseline near the bottom of the frame. Flying enemies should share a stable body center instead.

ARCHETYPE-SPECIFIC DIRECTION
Lantern Tosser — priority enemy. Hooded/crooked ranged nuisance carrying warm orange lanterns. Make the silhouette playful, sinister, and instantly identifiable. Orange glow must stay mundane; do NOT violet-tint the lantern flame. Cloth should be chunky and readable, not smoky painterly folds.

Frame specifics:
0 dead: collapsed robe/body with lantern extinguished or tipped nearby.
1 hurt/rise-react: recoils, lantern swings, body compressed by hit.
2 idle A: lantern held low, hunched watchful pose.
3 idle B: lantern lifted/checking the room; clearly different silhouette.
4 movement A: short shuffling step with lantern lag.
5 movement B: opposite step with cloak/lantern counter-swing.
8 movement C: continuation of the shuffle, opposite leg from frame 4, lanterns swinging further.
9 movement D: completes the stride from frame 5, cloak settling before the cycle repeats.
6 attack windup: winds arm back with lantern unmistakably prepared to throw.
7 attack release: fully extended toss pose; lantern has just left the hand or is at the edge of the frame.
